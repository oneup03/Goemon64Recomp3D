# Changelog

## 2026-11-11

### New
- **A proper backdrop for the bottom-screen HUD**: the pause screen's blue pattern now sits behind the status bar and map instead of plain black.
- **Space skies fill the whole view**: the starfield now carries on above and below instead of stopping at a hard edge partway up the screen.
- **Japanese voices (Redub) on by default** for new installs. Switch it off or pick the German translation on the Mods page; if you've already chosen, your choice is kept.
- Screenshots are numbered and never overwrite each other.

### Fixed
- The ending's space backdrop showing black blocks and stripes instead of the nebula.
- The 3D sky jumping around at the start of the space cutscene.
- The Impact cockpit going flat (2D) with the 3D on in parts of the battles.
- Flickering speckles where two surfaces overlap.

### Changed
- **The HOME quit prompt now quits with X instead of A**, so a press meant for the game's own menus can't quit by accident. B still goes back to the game.

## 2026-11-01

### New
- **HUD and map on the bottom screen**, so the top screen is all game. The castles get a map of the floor you're on (once you've found that castle's map). You can move it all back to the top screen in the settings.
- **Smooth camera on the C-Stick**: turn it freely and it stays put. Tap R to swing it back behind Goemon.
- **Seven built-in mods** on the new Mods page: Japanese voices (Redub), German translation, Boss Rush unlocked, Fast Travel, Custom Fish, Intro Lyrics and Attack While Moving.
- **Sound works out of the box**: no more dumping your DSP firmware.
- **Remap any button**, add up to two buttons on the touch screen, and set each screen's brightness.
- **Quitting from the HOME button keeps your save.**
- The Luma3DS Rosalina menu now works while playing.

### Fixed
- Music speeding up after playing for a while.
- A crash when closing the lid.
- Black bars or sky showing at the sides in the Impact battles.
- Transparent effects and weapon trails looking solid.
- Holes in models in one eye with the 3D on.
- The sky turning white on Old 3DS after playing a while.
- End credits photo corruption.

### Old 3DS / 2DS
- Faster loading with the CIA (extended memory mode: the console restarts when you quit).
- Smoother play: wireless and background services pause while the game runs, and the game gets a slightly larger share of the CPU.
